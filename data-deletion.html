How to delete your data

adsdesk and the Meta app “Jai Ads Manager” · Effective 24 November 2026

adsdesk keeps your data only on your own computer. Jai Wadhwa, who publishes it, does not receive or store your Meta data, so there is nothing on his side to delete. Deleting your data means two things: removing the app’s access in Meta, and deleting the files on your computer.

1. Remove the app’s access

Option A: from the tool. Run:

meta-control auth logout --revoke

This asks Meta to remove the app’s access to your account and then deletes the token from your keychain. It affects every device where you use adsdesk with that Facebook account.

Option B: in Facebook.

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Business integrations. (If “Jai Ads Manager” is not listed there, check Apps and websites.)
  3. Find Jai Ads Manager and choose Remove.

Either way, the app stops being able to reach your accounts through Meta straight away.

2. Delete the data on your computer

  1. Delete the stored token: meta-control auth logout (add --profile <name> for each extra profile you created). If you used Option A above, this is already done.
  2. Delete adsdesk’s folders. They hold your account list, change plans, the log of approved changes and any Instagram follower snapshots:
    SystemFolders
    macOS~/Library/Application Support/meta-control
    ~/Library/Caches/meta-control
    Linux~/.config/meta-control
    ~/.local/share/meta-control
    ~/.cache/meta-control
    Windows%LOCALAPPDATA%\meta-control
    Any system, older installs~/.config/meta-control
    If you set META_CONTROL_CONFIG_DIR, META_CONTROL_DATA_DIR or META_CONTROL_CACHE_DIR, delete those folders instead.
  3. Uninstall the software if you like: pipx uninstall meta-control-readonly or uv tool uninstall meta-control-readonly.

Reviewer console: it keeps the token only in the page’s memory and stores nothing in your browser. Closing the tab deletes it.

AI assistants: if you connected adsdesk to an AI assistant, anything you asked it to fetch is held by that assistant’s provider. Use that provider’s own deletion settings.

3. Ask Jai to confirm

You can email jai.w8a@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”. Jai will reply in writing within 30 days to confirm that he holds no Meta data about you, and will delete your email correspondence if you ask.

What this does not delete

Campaigns, ads, creatives and uploaded media that you created in your ad account belong to that ad account and are kept by Meta. You can delete them in Meta Ads Manager. Data that Meta itself holds about you is covered by Meta’s own policies and tools.
