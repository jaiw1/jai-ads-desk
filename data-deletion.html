How to delete your data

Jai Ads Desk · Last updated 5 November 2026

1. Remove the app's access in Facebook

  1. Open Facebook and go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites (it may be under Your activity or Security).
  3. Find Jai Ads Desk and choose Remove.

This stops the app from accessing your account through Meta straight away.

2. Ask for the local records to be deleted

Email jai.w8a@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and say which Facebook account, Page or ad account it concerns. Jai will delete the related tokens and local records from his computer and confirm by email, normally within 30 days.

What is not deleted this way

Ads, campaigns and other objects inside a Meta ad account belong to that ad account and are kept by Meta. The account owner can remove them in Meta Ads Manager.
