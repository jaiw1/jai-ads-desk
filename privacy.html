Privacy policy

Jai Ads Desk · Last updated 5 November 2026

Who runs it

Jai Ads Desk is operated by Jai Wadhwa, an individual (not a company). Contact: jai.w8a@gmail.com.

What it is

A personal tool Jai uses on his own computer to operate the Meta ad accounts he manages for his clients. It connects to Meta's Marketing API only with access that the account owners have granted to Jai in Meta. It is not a public service and nobody else signs in to it.

What information it handles

  • The access token Meta issues when Jai signs in with Facebook, his Meta user ID and the permissions Meta reports as granted.
  • Information about the ad accounts, Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts Jai has been given access to: account names and IDs, campaigns, ad sets, ads, creatives, budgets, targeting settings, performance figures and billing status.
  • Media and ad copy that Jai uploads to those ad accounts for his clients.

It does not ask for or store anyone's Facebook password, and it does not collect information from people who see the ads.

How it is used

Only to view and manage the ad accounts Jai works on: reading account data, preparing changes, and making changes that Jai has reviewed and approved.

Where it is stored

On Jai's own Mac. Access tokens and the app secret are kept in the macOS Keychain. Account IDs and a log of the changes made are kept in private local files readable only by Jai's user account. There is no separate server or cloud database for this tool. Requests go directly to Meta over HTTPS.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone else. Information is sent only to Meta, as part of using Meta's API. Jai may discuss an account's results with the business that owns that account.

How long it is kept

Only as long as Jai manages the related account. When he stops, or when you ask, the related local records and tokens are deleted.

Your choices

You can remove Jai Ads Desk's access at any time in Facebook, or ask for deletion by email. See How to delete your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change.
