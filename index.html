adsdesk

Your Meta ads and Instagram, from your own computer.

adsdesk is free, open-source software (MIT licence) for people who run Meta ads. It is a command-line tool, plus an MCP server so you can use it from an AI assistant you choose. It runs entirely on your computer.

  • Read: ad accounts, campaigns, ad sets, ads, creatives, performance reports, billing status, Pages, and Instagram profile, posts and metrics.
  • Change, with your approval: build campaigns, edit names, budgets and status, add ads, upload media. Every change is shown as an exact plan and runs only after you type its confirmation phrase. New campaigns are created paused.
  • Read-only mode: start it with --read-only and it refuses every change.

Private by design

  • Your data goes from Meta to your computer and nowhere else. The publisher receives nothing.
  • Your login token stays in your computer’s keychain.
  • No account to create, no analytics, no tracking.

Read the privacy policy and how to delete your data.
